Daily work from the CLI: `submissions ls|show|files|stats|export|update` for the form inbox (one result per ID, safe to rerun, visitor content marked untrusted), `analytics` and `links` for website traffic, Google search phrases and short links, `preview` to check and control the preview server, and `hosting previews` for preview builds and share links. Durations also accept days (`7d`).
